Over the past quarter our team has focused on improving the reliability of the order processing service, which had become the most common source of customer complaints. We started by adding detailed monitoring to every step of the checkout flow, from the moment a basket is submitted until the confirmation email is sent. The data quickly showed that most failures were caused by timeouts when calling the external payment provider during peak hours.

To address this, we introduced a queue between the checkout service and the payment gateway, so that short outages no longer cause orders to be lost. We also added automatic retries with increasing delays and a clear message for customers when a payment takes longer than expected. Since these changes went live six weeks ago, the number of failed orders has dropped by more than seventy percent, and support tickets about missing confirmations have almost disappeared.

There is still work to do. The queue needs better tooling for operators, who currently have to inspect stuck messages by hand, and we want to test how the system behaves when the payment provider is unavailable for more than an hour. In the next quarter we plan to build a small dashboard for the support team, run a full failover exercise, and share the lessons learned with the other product teams.
